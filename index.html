Reinforce · Live knowledge base

Policy Hub.

A searchable knowledge base for agents on live chats, emails and calls. It brings three policies into one structure, with decision flows, copy-ready replies and a monthly Knowledge Check.

Policies
Returns, Warranty & Repair, Verification
Content
33 sections, 49 quick answers
Tools
4 decision flows, 22 copy-ready replies
Knowledge Check
11 scenarios in 6 question types

Search the way customers ask.

Type "scanned 3 weeks ago, still no refund". It reads the day count, matches synonyms and typos, and opens the rule that applies.

Decide in two or three taps.

Each decision flow asks the questions an agent would ask, then shows the action and the reply to send.

Replies sit under their rule.

All 22 replies link to the section they follow, with customer details left in [brackets] to fill in.

A monthly Knowledge Check.

Live tickets where the customer reacts, plus spot-the-slip, put-in-order and match questions. Every answer links back to its rule.

Built with AI, checked against the source.

Made with Claude from the policy decks. Where a deck leaves a case open, the article says so and tells agents to ask their Team Leader.

Edited by the team, in place.

On the live version, editors update a section, a reply or a quiz question, and it is live for everyone with no republish.

Try it here. Search a case, run a decision flow or take the Knowledge Check.
